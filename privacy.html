Chord Creatures privacy

Chord Creatures is a family guitar-practice app. This page is the policy for the App Store listing.

Who the account is for

The email and password belong to a parent or teacher. Children do not create their own logins. A parent may add more than one child on the same phone, using a first name only so the creatures can say hello.

What we collect

  • Parent or teacher email, for signing in
  • A child’s first name, if the parent types one
  • Practice progress for each child (goals, locks, bonds)
  • Songs the family writes or saves
  • Microphone audio, only while practicing, to detect guitar chords on the device
  • Friend links a parent creates with a private invite code

Microphone

The microphone is used only to hear which guitar chords you play during practice. We do not use the mic to record conversations. Sound is processed to guess the chord; we do not sell recordings.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your information
  • We do not show ads
  • We do not advertise products to children
  • We do not put a store or prices on kid screens
  • We do not offer public chat between families
  • We do not connect two families unless a parent types a friend code they already have

Songs from friends

A song gift can wait for a parent to approve before a child sees it. Parents can turn that review on in Grown-ups.

Keeping and deleting data

Progress is stored on the phone and, if you sign in, in our database so it can return after a reinstall. You can skip sign-in and stay on the phone only.

Signed-in users can delete the account in Settings → Grown-ups → Delete account. That removes the login and cloud progress. You can also email us from the address on the App Store listing.

Contact

Use the email on the App Store listing for Chord Creatures.

This summary is for families using the app. It is not legal advice.
